## Content Rules Going Forward

### DO:
- Dark, moody backgrounds
- One product per image (or curated groupings)
- Natural or warm lighting
- Clean surfaces (black velvet, dark wood, marble)
- Tell the story of each piece in captions
- Use relevant hashtags (see below)

### DON'T:
- Hands in photos
- Cluttered backgrounds
- Multiple random items
- Flash photography
- Price in the caption (DM for price creates conversation)
